Bibleblazer

An offline study Bible for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Bibleblazer puts open-licence and public-domain Bibles, Greek and Hebrew word study, cross-references, classic commentaries and the Church Fathers on your device, with your own notes and highlights. Notes can sync through your own iCloud and be shared with people you invite.

Support

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue at github.com/anthonyazer1000/bibleblazer-site/issues. Please don't include anything private.

Sharing notes

Tap the Bibleblazer title, then Share notes with someone. Each person sends the other an invite. Notes marked Private stay yours.

Privacy

The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
