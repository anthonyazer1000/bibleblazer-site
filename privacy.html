Bibleblazer privacy policy

12 November 2026

Bibleblazer is a Bible study app by Anthony Azer. The short version: the app does not collect any data about you, and the developer receives none.

What stays on your device

Your notes, highlights, notebooks, tags, panel layout and settings are stored on your device. Bibleblazer has no accounts and no servers of its own.

iCloud (optional)

If you turn on iCloud, Bibleblazer keeps your notes and highlights in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices. That data belongs to you and is held by Apple under your Apple ID. The developer cannot see it.

If you choose to share your notes, they are shared only with the people you invite. Notes you mark Private are never shared, using Apple's CloudKit sharing. You can stop sharing at any time. The comments you and they write on each other's notes are shared the same way.

What we do not do

  • No analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking, and no data sold or shared with anyone.
  • No third-party software in the app that collects data about you.

Downloads

The Bible texts and study data are static files. On the web version they are downloaded the first time they are used and kept in your browser. The files are the same for everyone, and downloading them does not send anything about you.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/anthonyazer1000/bibleblazer-site/issues.
